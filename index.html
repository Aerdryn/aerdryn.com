About

AERDRYN LLC is a software company based in Washington State. We design, build and run custom software-as-a-service (SaaS) products, shaped around the people who use them.

Healthcare is a core focus: for healthcare clients, privacy and HIPAA compliance are built in from the start, and we work with each practice as its business associate. We also create products in other fields.
