1. Who we are

AERDRYN LLC ("AERDRYN", "we", "us") is a software company based in Washington State, USA. We design, build and run custom software-as-a-service (SaaS) products, including AirSync Nexus by Aerdryn for healthcare practices and Threads of Fate.

2. What this policy covers

This policy explains how we handle personal information:

  • when you visit aerdryn.com or the websites of our products;
  • when you create or use an account in one of our products;
  • when you contact us; and
  • when you agree to receive text messages (SMS) from us.

A product may have its own privacy notice. If it does, that notice applies in addition to this policy.

Patient health information

When a healthcare practice uses AirSync Nexus, AERDRYN acts as that practice's business associate under HIPAA. Patient health information inside AirSync Nexus is controlled by the practice and is governed by the practice's own Notice of Privacy Practices and by the business associate agreement between AERDRYN and the practice, not by this policy. We use and disclose it only as that agreement and the law allow. If you are a patient, please contact your practice with questions about your health information.

Please do not send health information to us through aerdryn.com or by email.

3. Information we collect

Account information

Some of our products let you create an account. For example, staff, providers and patients can have accounts in AirSync Nexus, and players create accounts in Threads of Fate. Depending on the product, your account is either hosted in the product or uses an outside sign-in (for example a Google Workspace account provided by your practice). We collect what is needed to run your account, such as your name, email address, sign-in details, your role, and the information you add to the product.

Usage information and cookies

When you use our websites and products, we and the services that host them receive technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages you visit. aerdryn.com is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts, so those providers receive this information when you load a page.

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, to remember your settings and, through analytics cookies, to understand how people use our products so we can fix problems and improve them.

We do not currently use advertising cookies. If we start using them, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some features, such as staying signed in, may not work without them.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write to us.

When you receive text messages from us

See section 5, "Text messaging (SMS)", below.

  • to provide, run and support our products and your account;

5. Text messaging (SMS)

We do not share or sell mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in consent information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

What we collect

  • your mobile phone number;
  • the messages we send you and any replies you send us, with their date and time; and
  • consent records: when and how you agreed to receive texts, and when you opted out.

How you consent

You agree to receive texts by completing an online consent form. You can withdraw your consent at any time by replying STOP to any message, by contacting your practice, or by emailing privacy@aerdryn.com.

Why we collect it

We use this information only to send you the messages you agreed to receive (such as account or service notifications, appointment-related messages and replies to your questions), to respond to your replies, to honor opt-out (STOP) and help (HELP) requests, and to keep a record of your consent.

Who processes it

Our phone and text messaging provider, Zoom Phone, and the mobile carriers process your phone number and messages only to deliver the messages. Apart from them, we share SMS information only if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your phone number, messages and consent records for 7 years after you opt out or our relationship ends, so we can show that we had your consent and that we honored your opt-out, and to meet our record-keeping obligations. We then delete them unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help. See our Terms of Service for the full SMS terms.

6. How we share information

We share personal information only:

  • with service providers that help us run our websites, products, analytics, email and messaging, and only so they can provide those services to us;
  • with the healthcare practice whose AirSync Nexus account you use;
  • if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or property of AERDRYN or others; or
  • as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of our business, in which case this policy continues to apply to the information transferred.

As stated in section 5, mobile phone numbers and SMS consent information are never shared for marketing or promotional purposes.

7. Security

8. Your choices

9. Children
